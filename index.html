Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Jordan, where I teach and research at the intersection of machine learning and cybersecurity.

Modern cyber-threat detection increasingly relies on complex ML models that defenders must be able to trust and understand. My research makes these models both powerful and transparent: I use eXplainable AI as a lens to probe, harden, and improve deep models, from XAI-guided adversarial training and deep ensembles (PANACEA) to counterfactual reasoning for data augmentation and oversampling (COBRA, CF-Aug). In some sense, my goal is to turn explanations from an after-thought into an engineering tool that makes intrusion and malware detection systems more robust.
